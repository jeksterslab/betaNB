Generates nonparametric bootstrap confidence intervals (Efron and
Tibshirani, 1993:
\<[doi:10.1201/9780429246593](https://doi.org/10.1201/9780429246593)\>)
for standardized regression coefficients (beta) and other effect sizes,
including multiple correlation, semipartial correlations, improvement in
R-squared, squared partial correlations, and differences in standardized
regression coefficients, for models fitted by lm().
